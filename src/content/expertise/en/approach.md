### Analysis and problem solving

I start from the question, not from the tool. Before choosing a model, I look for the simplest approach that could work and for the evidence that would prove it wrong. Curiosity does the rest: economics, finance, sports science, language models.

### Making complex ideas clear

Leading macroeconomics tutorials at the Toulouse School of Economics and training colleagues in R taught me to adapt the message to the audience. I present results to technical teams and to decision-makers alike, and I can explain a confidence interval without a single equation.

### Under pressure, as a team

Consulting missions for large companies come with deadlines and demanding stakeholders. I work well in a team: I ask for reviews, I give them, and I flag problems early rather than late.

### Attention to detail

Tests, reproducible pipelines, results that can be traced back to the code that produced them. Quality is not a final step; it is part of every step.
