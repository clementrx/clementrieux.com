### On the field

- **Javelin at national level**, with handball, swimming, boxing and strength training on the side.
- Competing teaches things no course does: preparing for the one throw that counts, accepting a bad day and coming back the following week.
- It is also where I learned that progress is rarely linear, which is a useful reminder when a model stops improving.

### Data at the service of training

- **Training load**: session RPE, volume and intensity, acute and chronic workload, monotony and strain.
- **Recovery**: heart rate variability, resting heart rate and sleep, read against the training load rather than in isolation.
- **Progress**: performance tests treated as measurements with their own noise, so a real improvement is not confused with a good day.
- **Planning**: periodisation and tapering choices grounded in published research.

Small samples, differences between individuals and the quality of wearable data call for caution: a trend is reported as a trend, never as a diagnosis.
