### What data can answer

- **Training load**: session RPE, volume and intensity, acute and chronic workload, monotony and strain.
- **Recovery**: heart rate variability, resting heart rate and sleep, read against the training load rather than in isolation.
- **Progress**: performance tests treated as measurements with their own noise, so a real improvement is not confused with a good day.
- **Planning**: periodisation and tapering choices grounded in published research.

### What I am careful about

Small samples, differences between individuals and the quality of wearable data. A trend is reported as a trend, never as a diagnosis.
